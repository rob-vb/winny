**As a** returning or progressing user, **I want** milestone and comeback moments to be acknowledged warmly after I log a win, **so that** the app encourages action instead of foregrounding misses.

<objective>

This plan completes the user-facing COPY-01 moment surface while preserving the fast Home input loop.
</objective>

| T-06-09 | Information Disclosure | Home banner | low | Banner copy is generic; no win text or personal goal is echoed |
