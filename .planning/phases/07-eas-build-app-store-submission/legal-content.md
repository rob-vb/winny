# Winny — Privacy Policy

Effective: [DATE]

## What Data Winny Collects

Winny stores your wins, streak count, reminder time, and display name locally on your device. No personal data is sent to our servers.

## Push Notifications

If you grant notification permission, iOS or Android schedules notifications locally on your device. No notification token is transmitted to Winny's servers.

## Third-Party Services

Winny uses Expo (expo.dev) for over-the-air updates. Expo may receive anonymous diagnostic information (e.g., crash reports). See expo.dev/privacy for Expo's policy.

## Data Not Collected

Winny does not collect your name, email, location, health data, or any other personal information. No analytics SDK is included.

## Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. There is no account to delete.
