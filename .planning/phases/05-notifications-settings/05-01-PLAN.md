## Phase Goal

**As a** daily habits user, **I want to** receive a daily push notification reminding me to log my wins, **so that** the app anchors my habit even when I'm not actively opening it.

<objective>
Slice 1 of 4: Notification Foundation.

After this plan executes, a user who saves their first win on an EAS dev build will see the OS permission dialog. If they grant, 30 notifications are scheduled at 8:00 PM local. Every subsequent foreground transition silently refreshes the queue.
