## Description

Winny is a simple daily habit app for noticing what is already going right.

Open the app, write one win, and keep moving. Your streak and win history turn small moments into visible proof of progress, without accounts, feeds, or guilt.

What you can do with Winny:

- Log a daily win in seconds
- Build a visible winning streak
- Review your past wins in your trophy room
- Add an optional goal to keep in view
- Set optional local reminders
- Keep everything local on your device

Winny is built for the quick moments: morning coffee, end-of-day reflection, after a workout, or anytime you need proof that progress is happening.

Just keep winning.

## App Review Notes

Winny does not require an account. Data is stored locally on the user's device. Notification permission is optional and is only used for user-configured daily reminders. The app remains fully usable if notification permission is denied.
